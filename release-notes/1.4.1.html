Pepper 1.4.1

  • SBS fonts for title cards. Opening titles and closing cards now use the SBS brand fonts by default: a bold headline with a Nantes second line. Pick SBS Brand, SBS Headline or Nantes from the top of the Font menu, or any other font as before.
  • Captions stay behind title cards. Captions and keystrokes no longer show on top of an opening or closing card; they fade in as the card fades out.

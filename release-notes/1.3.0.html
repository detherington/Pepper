Pepper 1.3

Pepper now requires macOS 26 or later.

  • Name your recordings. Right-click a recent recording in the main window and choose Rename. The name shows in the editor and becomes the default title when you send it to Orbis.
  • Teleprompter and Soundboard in the main window. No more hunting through the menu bar for them.
  • Less disk space. Pepper no longer makes an extra video file after every recording; you export or send to Orbis from the editor. Want the file anyway? Turn on "Also save a video file after each recording" in Settings.
  • Captions without a permission prompt. Captions are written on your Mac and never ask for Speech Recognition.

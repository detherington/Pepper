Pepper 1.4.0

  • Zoom the timeline. Use the magnifier buttons, View › Zoom In (⌘+), or pinch on the timeline to get a close look at long recordings. The timeline follows along as the video plays.
  • Cut by dragging. Hold Shift and drag across part of the timeline, then press Delete. To put a cut back, point at it and click its arrow.
  • See how long your video will be. The timeline shows the final length, after trimming and cuts.
  • Watch it your way. Click the video to play or pause, play at up to 2× to check it quickly, or watch it full screen with the button at the end of the timeline.
  • Fix a word everywhere. In Captions, fix a name the captions got wrong in every line at once.
  • Easier exports. Export shows how long is left, then where your video was saved, with Show in Finder and Copy to paste it into Slack or Mail.
  • And more. The editor opens at the start of your video, Delete removes the selected zoom, Home and End jump to the start and end, error messages are clearer, and the installer has a new look.
